The big idea

Plants make their own food using sunlight. They take in water from the soil and a gas called carbon dioxide from the air. Their leaves turn these ingredients into sugar, which gives the plant energy.

✦ Simple English

Remember these

  • 01 Plants use sunlight to make food.
  • 02 Leaves take in carbon dioxide from the air.
  • 03 Water comes up from the roots.

Quick practice

  1. What do plants use sunlight for?
  2. Where does a plant get its water?
